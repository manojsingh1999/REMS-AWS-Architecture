## Major cost drivers (prod)

1. **Aurora + RDS Proxy** (≈ $5.4k, 36 % of prod and 48 % of prod infrastructure): 3 × r7g.2xlarge I/O-Optimized (core) plus 2 × r7g.xlarge (rag). I/O-Optimized was chosen because REMS is write-heavy (events, incidents, vectors), and I/O charges would otherwise exceed about 25 % of DB spend. Validate this with real I/O metrics after the first month: switching back to Standard is allowed once every 30 days. This is the first place to look for savings (RIs, right-sizing after k6).
2. **EKS compute + EBS** (≈ $2.9k, 19 % of prod): the observability pool (VictoriaMetrics, Loki, Tempo, Prometheus) is the largest single node pool. That's inherent to an observability product.
3. **Bedrock** (≈ $3.8k, 25 % of prod, the most *variable* driver): it scales with tenants and usage. It's bounded by per-tenant budgets in the LLM gateway and passed through in pricing.
4. **ElastiCache** (6 %), **network** (NAT, VPC endpoints, ALB, data transfer: 7 %), plus WAF requests and GuardDuty make up the long tail.
5. The **DR site** adds about 30 % on top of prod, the price of warm standby for RTO 2 h. Pilot light (no EKS nodes, Aurora headless) would cut it to about 12 %, but the RTO would move towards 3–4 h.

## Per-tenant unit economics

At 100 tenants, prod costs roughly **$110 per tenant per month** for shared infrastructure plus **$40 per tenant per month** of AI at the assumed usage, before
optimizations. Shared costs fall per tenant as tenants are added (shared clusters, sublinear scaling of the control plane, security services and DR). AI cost is linear
in usage and is capped per tenant by the gateway budgets.
